About Dead Letter Studios

A solo indie journey dedicated to classic survival horror, internet folklore, and analog psychological terror.

One Developer. Unfiltered Nightmares.

Dead Letter Studios is run entirely by a solo independent game developer passionate about recapturing the raw, unpolished, and intensely unsettling nature of retro survival horror.

Named after letters that can never be delivered, every game is a love letter to the late-90s golden age of horror—from the static-choked halls of PSX survival classics to modern creepypasta mythologies like Slenderman and Japanese folklore icons like Hachishakusama.

"Dare to dream, to innovate, and to create a future where the only limit is the extent of our imagination."
